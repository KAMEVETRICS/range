
## 8. Honest release gate and next venue expansion

The current reviewed mapping count is zero, and the connectors' `main.ts` scripts are probes. Therefore the first VPS milestone is **infrastructure + tested runtime**, not an arbitrage demo. Require all of the following before publishing a live opportunity:

1. Continuous Bitget and at least two other connector processes are healthy through the same event contract.
2. At least one canonical pair is independently reviewed using live product metadata: multiplier, settlement/collateral, market schedule, and economic exposure; the mapping version is recorded.
3. Both books have executable depth with proven sequencing; funding has actual settlement semantics; all source clocks/freshness are within budgets.
4. The opportunity is produced by the worker from real source events, survives evidence/revision checks through REST/MCP, and expires on stale/gap fault injection.
5. The unsigned intent has bounded size/TTL, fresh validation, no secret or signing fields, and an auditable caller/idempotency record.

If any gate fails, serve `partial`/`rejected` with named reasons and show a synthetic fixture only as a clearly labeled mechanics demonstration. Post-MVP connector order and per-venue gates are in the original plan; do not add QFEX, Lighter, Aster, etc. to actionable output merely to increase venue count.
